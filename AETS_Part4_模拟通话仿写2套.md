# AETS Part 4 模拟陆空通话 仿写脚本（第 2–3 套）

> 角色说明：CCA/CSN/CES/AFR/CHH/JAL 等行 = 飞行员通话（预录音频，考生听）；
> **B:** = 给考生的指令提词（按此组织通话）；**C:** = 参考答案（标准通话）。
> 地理沿用虚构武汉体系：RWY36R；KG-11A / XS-11A / DA-01A 进场；OBLIK、ZF、KG、DA、TM、XS、WHA、HZ；APP 118.9、TWR 124.35。

# Simulation 2（主线特情：发动机火警 MAYDAY）

**Directions: In this part, you are going to play the role of WH ATC, listen to the recordings and follow the instructions in the prompt. Record your answers within the time limit.**

CSN: WUHAN CONTROL, CSN4580, from SHANGHAI to CHENGDU, maintaining 9800m on standard, passing TM 14:10, estimating WHA 14:25.
B: Inform CSN4580 that you have seen the A/C on the radar, and instruct to continue present level, and call you when over WHA.
C: CSN4580, WUHAN CONTROL, radar contact, maintain level, report WHA.
CSN: maintain 9800m, I'll call you when passing WHA, CSN4580.
AFR: WUHAN CONTROL, AFR668 from GUANGZHOU to WUHAN, 7500m maintaining, NOW over ZF. Estimating over KG at 33, request descend.
B: Inform that you have seen the A/C on radar, tell to lower his level to 5700m, and call you when passing KG.
C: AFR668, WUHAN CONTROL, radar contact, descend to 5700m, report KG.
AFR: descend to and maintain 5700m, and I'll call you when passing KG, AFR668.
B: WH app just informs you that there will be some delays at WH due to traffic in the terminal area, tell AFR668 to put off the time to pass KG for 10min.
C: AFR668, CTL, due to traffic, delay KG time for 10min.
AFR: negative, AFR668.
B: Tell AFR668 that it will hold over KG.
C: AFR668, ROGER, expect hold at KG.
AFR: Roger, AFR668.
AFR: WUHAN CONTROL, AFR668, now 5700m maintaining.
B: Inform AFR668 that you have received the message, instruct him to lower his level to 4500m, hold at KG at this level, call you when over KG.
C: AFR668, CONTROL, roger, continue descend to 4500m, hold at KG, report KG.
AFR: descend to and maintain 4500m, and hold at KG at 4500m, report passing KG, AFR668.
CSN: MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY, WUHAN CONTROL, CSN4580, our right engine caught fire, the fire is extinguished now, request landing at WUHAN. Position 20km east of WHA and 9800m.
B: Inform CSN4580 that you have received his distress message, and tell him to lower its level to 5100m, and call you when passing WHA.
C: CSN4580, WUHAN CONTROL, roger MAYDAY, descend to 5100m, report WHA.
CSN: descend to 5100m, and report passing WHA, CSN4580.
B: Ask CSN4580 to confirm the fire is completely out, and get some info about injuries and damage.
C: CSN4580, confirm the fire is completely extinguished, any injuries and damage?
CSN: WUHAN CONTROL, the fire is completely out, no injuries, but the engine is vibrating seriously.
B: Inform CSN4580 that you have received this message, get some info of the NO of people on board and the assistance they need on landing.
C: CSN4580, roger, confirm POB and the assistance you need on landing.
CSN: We have 155 passengers and 9 crew members on board, request fire trucks on standby.
B: Inform CSN4580 that you have received the message, tell them the fire trucks will be ready for the arrival.
C: CSN4580, roger, the fire trucks will be ready.
AFR: WUHAN CONTROL, AFR668, 4500m maintaining, now over KG.
B: Tell AFR668 to hold over KG according to the published procedure, the holding level is 4500m, and inform them that they will receive the next instructions at 25.
C: AFR668, maintain 4500m, hold at KG as published, expect further instruction at 25.
AFR: hold at KG, 4500m, AFR668.
CSN: WUHAN CONTROL, CSN4580, 5100m maintaining, now passing WHA.
B: Tell CSN4580 to establish radio comms with WH APP on 118.9.
C: CSN4580, WUHAN CONTROL, contact WUHAN APPROACH on 118.9.
CSN: contact APPROACH 118.9, CSN4580.
B: Now, you are in WH APP. Inform CSN4580 that you have seen him on the radar, inform CSN4580 that the latest info is M.
C: CSN4580, WH APP, radar contact, roger your situation. Info M.
CSN: Roger information M, CSN4580.
B: Inform CSN4580, you will provide RV service, app procedure is ILS app, the RWY in use is 36R, instruct the pilot to set heading to 200, and lower lvl to 2100m at 1009 hectopascals.
C: CSN4580, WUHAN APPROACH, radar vector for ILS approach RWY36R, fly heading 200, descend to 2100m on QNH 1009.
CSN: heading 200, ILS approach RWY36R, descend to 2100m on QNH 1009, CSN4580.
CCA: WUHAN CONTROL, CCA2290, 5700m descending to 5100m, estimating DA 40.
B: Tell CCA2290 to maintain 5100m and call you when passing DA.
C: CCA2290, WUHAN CONTROL, maintain 5100m, report passing DA.
CCA: Roger, maintain 5100m, I'll call you when passing DA, CCA2290.
B: Tell AFR668 to leave the holding procedure now, and establish radio comms with WH APP 118.9.
C: AFR668, leave holding procedure, contact WUHAN APPROACH on 118.9, good day.
AFR: leave holding procedure, WUHAN APPROACH 118.9, AFR668.
AFR: WUHAN APPROACH, AFR668, 4500m maintaining, NOW passing KG, information M.
B: Inform AFR668 that you have seen him on radar, tell to proceed to the A/P via KG11A, the app procedure will be ILS app, lower lvl to 2700m, and the QNH is 1009.
C: AFR668, WUHAN APPROACH, radar contact, follow KG-11A, expect ILS approach RWY36R, descend to 2700m on QNH 1009.
AFR: follow KG-11A, ILS approach RWY36R, descend to and maintain 2700m on QNH 1009, AFR668.
B: Tell AFR668 to slow down its speed to 240kts, because there is not enough room. Inform that AFR668 is No.2 in the app sequence.

AFR: speed 240kts, No.2 for app, AFR668.
CCA: WUHAN CONTROL, CCA2290, 5100m maintaining, now passing DA.
B: Ask CCA2290 to establish radio comms with WH App on 118.9.
C: CCA2290, WUHAN CONTROL, contact WUHAN APPROACH on 118.9.
CCA: WUHAN APPROACH, 118.9, CCA2290.
CCA: WUHAN APPROACH, CCA2290, 5100m maintaining, now passing DA.
B: Inform CCA2290 that you have seen him on radar, tell CCA2290 to proceed to A/P via DA-01A, the app procedure will be ILS app, RWY in use is 36R, tell CCA2290 to lower its lvl to 3000m on QNH 1009.
C: CCA2290, WUHAN APPROACH, radar contact, follow DA-01A, expect ILS approach RWY36R, descend to 3000m on QNH 1009.
CCA: DA-01A, ILS approach RWY36R, descend to 3000m on QNH 1009, CCA2290.
B: Tell CSN4580 to slow down to 170kts, lower to 1200m, inform CSN4580 is No.1 in app sequence.
C: CSN4580, reduce speed to 170kts, descend to 1200m, you're NO.1.
CSN: reduce speed to 170kts, descend to and maintain 1200m, CSN4580.
B: Ask CSN4580 to make a right turn, set course on 250 degrees and heading for base.
C: CSN4580, turn right heading 250 for base.
CSN: roger, heading 250, CSN4580.
B: Ask CSN4580 to turn right to a heading of 320, tell CSN4580 that he is allowed to capture the localizer signal of RWY36R, and call you when the signal is captured.
C: CSN4580, turn right heading 320, cleared for ILS approach RWY36R, report established localizer.
CSN: roger, heading 320 to intercept the localizer of RWY36R, CSN4580.
CSN: WUHAN APPROACH, CSN4580, established the localizer.
B: Inform CSN4580 that he is 12km from touchdown, establish radio comms with WH TWR on 124.35.
C: CSN4580, 12km from touchdown, radar service terminated, contact WH tower on 124.35.
CSN: contact tower on 124.35, CSN4580.
CSN: MAYDAY MAYDAY MAYDAY, WUHAN TOWER, this is CSN4580, single engine, 9km from touchdown, fully established on RWY36R.
B: Inform CSN4580 that you have received his distress message, ask him to call you when passing OM, tell the pilot that fire trucks are ready for him.
C: CSN4580, WUHAN TOWER, roger MAYDAY, report passing outer marker, fire trucks are ready.
CSN: roger, report outer marker, CSN4580.
CSN: outer marker, CSN4580.
B: Issue landing clearance to CSN4580, the wind component is 020 at 6 meters/sec, gusting to 13m/s, and ask if he needs a tug.
